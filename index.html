Thank you for coming to
An Experiment on the Possibility of Sharing Private Affect through Cooking Gestures
— A Body Fidgeting Between Emptiness (空) and Togetherness (共)
Tap here to go to the next page.
This project began as a curiosity about empathy.

We wondered: if artists in New York and Korea—strangers to one another—share their own “soul recipes,” and each person cooks the other’s recipe, could we sense who the other is, and what kinds of memories are held inside the food?

HOW THIS WEB APP WORKS
By default, you can watch the stage video used during the performance.

If you wish, tap SHARE MY MEMORY to upload an image of your soul food from your photo library and write a short memory connected to it.

Or tap SHARE MY FEELING to write and share any thoughts or feelings that arise while watching.
